01 / Workflow

From raw media to a reviewable brief.

The news-editor profile guides the process. When information is missing or a source cannot be verified, the Agent should say so and ask the requester for direction.

01

Understand the request

Identify the goal, scope, input, desired format, and recipient.

02

Check the source

Prefer official sources; flag conflicting or unverified information.

03

Extract key points

Summarize relevant findings, retain source links, and do not widen the requested time range or count.

04

Review and deliver

Check the content, status, recipient, and sharing scope before handoff.

02 / Standards

Clear sources. Clear confidence.

The brief should separate supported information from gaps. A tool reporting completion does not replace a content review.

A useful brief includes

Enough detail to verify

  • A task summary and a status: Completed, In Progress, or Failed.
  • Key points, timestamps when supported, and source links.
  • Clear notes on uncertainty, access limits, or next steps.
Editorial principles

Do not fill gaps with guesses

  • Do not invent or distort information to reach a requested count.
  • Do not present old items as new or silently change the time range.
  • If reliable sources conflict and evidence is insufficient, separate the issue and ask for guidance.
03 / Capability boundaries

Supports editorial work. Does not replace human review.

Actual results depend on access, media quality, and the tools available to the Hermes Agent.

What it can help with

  • Review accessible audio/video material or URLs.
  • Extract notable points within the requested topic and scope.
  • Draft a concise brief with sources and notes about uncertainty.
  • Reply through Feishu in a configured conversation.

What not to assume

  • Verbatim transcription, speaker identification, or accurate subtitles for every format.
  • Access to every URL, login-protected file, long recording, or untested format.
  • Independent resolution of vague requests, suspicious sources, or unclear sharing rights.
  • Replacement of human approval before important content is published.
